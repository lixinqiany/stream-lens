# Store listing copy / 商店介绍文案

Ready to copy into separate English and Simplified Chinese listings. The extension has not been submitted to the Chrome Web Store.

## English

**Name:** StreamLens

**Summary:** Keep the videos you love. Select a player, choose your quality, and save web videos straight to disk.

**Description:**

Keep a local copy of the web videos you enjoy, with controls right on the player and a compact download sidebar.

- Select the player you want. Other autoplaying videos do not change your selection.
- Discover available quality options automatically and choose the one you prefer.
- Pick a filename and location before downloading. Videos stream to disk as they download.
- Pause, resume, retry, and manage your downloads in one place.
- Use English or Simplified Chinese, with light and dark appearance options.

Supported formats include compatible HLS on-demand streams, DASH audio/video tracks, and direct MP4 / WebM files. Compatibility depends on the site, encoding, and your normal playback access. DRM-protected videos and live recording are not supported.

Videos are processed locally. No telemetry or cloud uploads. Download only content you have permission to save.

## 简体中文

**名称：** 拾影 StreamLens

**简介：** 喜欢的视频，值得留下。轻巧的网页视频下载工具，自动识别所选播放器，自选画质，边下边存。

**介绍：**

把喜欢的网页视频留在本地。从播放器快捷按钮或简洁侧栏开始下载，随时查看和管理任务。

- 点选想下载的播放器，其他自动播放的视频不会改变选择。
- 自动识别可用清晰度，按需选择画质。
- 下载前选择文件名和位置，边下载边写入磁盘。
- 集中管理任务，支持暂停、继续、重试与筛选。
- 支持简体中文与英文，以及深浅两种外观。

支持兼容的 HLS 点播、DASH 音视频和 MP4 / WebM 直链。具体可用性取决于网站、编码与正常播放权限。不支持 DRM 保护的视频和直播录制。

视频仅在本机处理，无遥测、无云端上传。请仅保存你有权下载的内容。
